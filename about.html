What is DealRadar?

DealRadar is a price comparison tool that helps travelers compare Delhi Duty Free prices with Indian market prices before they fly. By providing live price tracking and clear savings calculations, DealRadar takes the guesswork out of airport shopping.

How it works

  1. We check prices: Duty Free prices are matched with the latest available market references.
  2. You see the difference: Each product shows its price gap and a simple deal score at a glance.
  3. You buy smarter: Take the guesswork out of your airport shopping list.

Data Sources

Duty Free prices are sourced from the Delhi Duty Free official GraphQL API (live, updated daily). Market prices are gathered from various Indian retailers and e-commerce platforms.
