A smarter shopping list

What is DealRadar?

DealRadar helps you compare Delhi Duty Free prices with Indian market references before you travel. Browse perfumes, liquor, chocolates, cosmetics and accessories, then check the official listing when something catches your eye.

How the comparison works

  1. Start with the saved price. The catalogue displays the most recently collected Duty Free prices. Check the update date above the filters; this is a saved snapshot.
  2. Compare the reference. Where a market reference is available, we subtract the Duty Free price to show the potential saving. A negative saving means the market reference is cheaper.
  3. Use the score as a guide. The percentage saving is multiplied by 2.5, rounded and capped between 0 and 100. A higher score means a larger saving against that reference. Unrated means there is no market comparison; an airport exclusive does not automatically earn a score.

“Total savings across matches” adds the positive savings for one of each matched listing in your current results. It is a catalogue total, and includes different sizes and variants.

Where prices come from

Delhi Duty Free

Prices are collected from the official Delhi Duty Free catalogue. Each product card links to its store listing where available.

Market references

References come from the project’s retail and liquor MRP catalogue. Source links are shown where available. Reference dates and product matches may vary.

A useful guide, with a final check

Prices, promotions and availability can change. Confirm the exact product, pack size, concentration and current price with the retailer before purchasing. Market references may be older than the Duty Free snapshot.

DealRadar is an independent comparison tool and is not affiliated with Delhi Duty Free or the retailers referenced.
