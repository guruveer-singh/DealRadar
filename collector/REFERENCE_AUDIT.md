# DealRadar — Reference Price Audit & Change Log

This file tracks all market reference prices, their verification sources, pricing methodology, and an immutable log of every change made to the reference database.

## 1. Reference Pricing Principles & Rules

1. **Domestic Availability Rule**:
   - A market reference price MUST come from official Indian domestic retail channels (Nykaa, Sephora India, Parcos, Shoppers Stop, Amazon.in Official/Appario, Tata CLiQ Luxury, Delhi Excise Board).
   - **Travel Retail / Airport Exclusives** (e.g., *Lindt Dubai Style Dark Chocolate*, airport miniature gift sets, special travel-exclusive cask editions) that have no official domestic Indian retail release are **excluded** from synthetic price comparisons to prevent false savings or distorted "costlier than market" alerts.

2. **Unit & Size Parity**:
   - Prices must be compared against the matching volume/size (e.g., 50 ml vs 50 ml, 100 ml vs 100 ml, 1 L vs 1 L).
   - Miniature sets (e.g., 4 x 5 ml) must never be compared against a single 100 ml retail bottle without explicit bundle reference.

3. **Liquor Fixed MRP**:
   - Liquor in India is subject to state excise laws where MRP is the legally enforced maximum retail price with zero discounts.
   - Delhi Excise Board Gazette / Official Portal is the single source of truth for liquor market baselines.
